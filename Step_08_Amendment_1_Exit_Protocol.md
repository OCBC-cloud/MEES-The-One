## MEES - The One
### Amendment 1 to Step 08: Exit Protocol
**Status:** RATIFIED (Provisionally adopted by Founding Steward)  
**Effective Date:** Immediately  
**Purpose:** To address Kimi's valid critique that exit rights are the ultimate check on institutional overreach. This amendment establishes clear, dignified exit pathways for both participants and Microclimates.

---

### 1. Amendment to Step 08, Section 3 (Participant Recruitment and Consent)

**Add the following clause after Section 3.2:**

#### 3.3 Participant Exit Rights

Any participant may withdraw from a Microclimate at any time, for any reason, with no penalty.

**The Withdrawal Process:**

1. **Notification:** The participant submits a written or digital notice to the local stewards.
2. **Data Handling (Option A — Anonymized Retention):** The participant's historical data remains in the Regenerative Ledger but is permanently anonymized (all personally identifiable information removed).
3. **Data Handling (Option B — Full Deletion):** The participant may request that their data be fully deleted from the ledger. This request is honored, but the participant must acknowledge that:
   - Deleting data may affect the integrity of historical RCI calculations.
   - The ledger will record that data was deleted (with a timestamp) but will not store the data itself.
4. **Renewal Credits:** Any unspent Renewal Credits held by the exiting participant are forfeited (they return to the common pool).
5. **Legacy Debt:** Any frozen or outstanding legacy debt is recorded as a "withdrawal adjustment" and is absorbed by the Microclimate's common renewal fund.
6. **Exit Interview (Optional):** The participant is invited (but not required) to provide feedback on their experience. This feedback is published to the public log (anonymized).

**Protection Against Retaliation:** No participant shall be penalized, shamed, or disadvantaged for withdrawing. Any such behavior by stewards or other participants shall be grounds for immediate review under the Conflict Resolution Protocol (Step 10 Amendment).

### 2. Amendment to Step 08, Section 1 (Microclimate Principle)

**Add the following clause after the existing text:**

#### 1.1 Microclimate Exit Rights

A Microclimate may exit MEES under the following conditions:

| Condition | Process |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Participant Vote** | If ≥⅔ of active participants vote to exit, the Microclimate may dissolve. |
| **SDS Oversight** | If SDS determines that the Microclimate is materially violating the Constitution, SDS may initiate a dissolution process (subject to participant appeal). |
| **Automatic Dissolution** | If RCI < 40 for two consecutive years, the Microclimate is automatically dissolved (the system has functionally collapsed). |

**The Microclimate Dissolution Process:**

1. **Notice:** A public notice is issued to the canonical record and the International Invitation Protocol (Step 11).
2. **Ledger Archival:** The Microclimate's Regenerative Ledger is archived (with full historical data) and published to the public log.
3. **Asset Distribution:** Any remaining communal assets (Renewal Credits, infrastructure, funds) are distributed to participants (prorated by participation).
4. **Debt Settlement:** Outstanding legacy debt is either:
   - Absorbed by the MEES central fund (if available), or
   - Negotiated with external creditors (with full transparency).
5. **Final Report:** A comprehensive report is published: what worked, what failed, what changed, why.
6. **Participant Absorption:** Exiting participants are invited to join other Microclimates (if any exist) or to remain as observers.

**Protection Against Malicious Dissolution:** A Microclimate cannot be dissolved simply because the stewards disagree with the Constitution. The participant vote threshold (≥⅔) and the automatic dissolution threshold (RCI < 40) provide objective, non-arbitrary criteria.

---

### 3. Rationale for This Amendment

Kimi correctly noted that exit rights are the ultimate check on institutional overreach. Without an exit pathway, participants and Microclimates are effectively trapped—which breeds resentment, distrust, and eventual collapse.

By establishing:

- **Unconditional participant exit** (no penalty, no retaliation),
- **Clear data handling** (anonymized retention vs. full deletion),
- **Microclimate dissolution pathways** (vote, oversight, or automatic collapse),
- **Asset and debt settlement rules**,

MEES becomes a system that participants choose to join and stay in—not one they are trapped in. This is a fundamental trust-builder.
