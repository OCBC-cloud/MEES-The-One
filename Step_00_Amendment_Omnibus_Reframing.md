## MEES - The One
### Omnibus Amendment: Reframing from Experimental to Constitutional
**Status:** PROVISIONALLY ADOPTED (Subject to 90-day commentary period)  
**Date:** 2026-08-31  
**Purpose:** To formally remove all references to experimental testing, pilot projects, and field deployments. MEES is hereby reframed as a constitutional framework for intellectual contribution and peer review—not a testing ground for theories.

---

### 1. Principles of Reframing

- MEES does **not** conduct experiments on communities.
- MEES does **not** require pilot testing to be credible.
- MEES invites intellectual contribution, challenge, and refinement—not participation in a simulation.
- Communities may choose to adopt the framework voluntarily; MEES will not solicit or recruit participants for testing.
- The only formal process is the **90-day commentary period** established in Step 00 (Notice of Provisional Adoption).

---

### 2. Amendments to Specific Steps

#### 2.1 Step 04 (System Model)

**Replace all references to "Microclimate 001" and "experimental deployment" with the following:**

> "The MEES System Model is a proposal for how a community *could* organize its economy if it chooses to adopt the framework. Adoption is voluntary and occurs only when a community decides to do so. MEES does not initiate, fund, or manage any such adoption. The framework exists as a public good—available for use, challenge, and improvement by anyone."

**Remove:** Section 3 (The Microclimate Blueprint), Section 4 (The Relational Health Index), Section 5 (The Connection Between Microclimates), and Section 7 (The Learning Loop). These sections implied active experimentation.

**Replace with:** A single statement:

> "MEES offers its constitutional framework to the world. Any community may adopt, adapt, or ignore it. MEES will not track or verify such adoptions. Its role is to maintain the canonical record and respond to intellectual challenges."
