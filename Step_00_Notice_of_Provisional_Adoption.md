### 1. Acknowledgment of Procedural Irregularity

On 2026-08-31, nine (9) amendments to the MEES constitutional framework were provisionally adopted by the Founding Steward. These amendments addressed critical vulnerabilities identified in the first Kimi Red-Team review.

It is hereby acknowledged that these amendments were adopted **without** the 90-day commentary period and supermajority vote required by Article VI of the Constitution.

This was not done in bad faith. The Founding Steward acted to rapidly close severe structural vulnerabilities (SDS succession, RCI fragility, lack of exit rights, etc.) that could have rendered MEES inoperable if left unaddressed. The amendments were adopted provisionally—not permanently—with the intent to open a formal commentary period as soon as possible.

However, as Kimi correctly noted, *"You cannot build a rule-of-law system by breaking its rules in the founding moment."*

This notice corrects that error.

### 3. How to Participate in the Commentary Period

**Who is a participant during this pre-Microclimate phase?**  
During the pre-Microclimate phase, any human who has submitted a public comment to the GitHub repository (via Issue or Pull Request) is considered a provisional participant for the purpose of this commentary period.

**How to participate:**

| Action | How |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Endorse** an amendment | Submit a public statement to the canonical record (via GitHub Issue or pull request) |
| **Challenge** an amendment | Submit a written challenge with evidence and rationale (via GitHub Issue or pull request) |
| **Propose modifications** | Submit a modified version of the amendment (via pull request) |
| **Abstain** | No action required |

**Deadline:** All commentary must be received by **2026-11-29** (90 days from the date of this notice).

---

### 4. Ratification Process

At the conclusion of the 90-day commentary period:

1. The Founding Steward (or Interim Stewardship Council, if applicable) shall publish a summary of all commentary received.
2. A formal vote shall be held. All active participants (as defined in Step 10) are eligible to vote.
3. Each amendment requires a supermajority (≥⅔) of votes cast to be permanently ratified.
4. Ratified amendments become permanent. Amendments that fail ratification are archived as historical drafts.

**If no formal challenges are received during the commentary period**, the amendments shall be considered ratified by consensus at the end of the 90-day period.

---

### 5. Status of the Amendments During the Commentary Period

During the 90-day commentary period, the amendments remain in **provisional effect**. They are operational but subject to reversal or modification based on the commentary and vote.

This is analogous to a provisional constitution or interim measure—necessary for the system to function while legitimacy is being established.

---

### 6. Rationale for This Notice

This notice addresses Kimi's valid critique that the amendments violated Article VI. By:

- Acknowledging the procedural irregularity,
- Opening the 90-day commentary period retroactively,
- Establishing a clear ratification process,
- Allowing for challenges, endorsements, and modifications,

MEES demonstrates that it is **self-correcting**—not because it is perfect, but because it has mechanisms to acknowledge and repair its own imperfections.

This is the operational expression of Constitutional Article I, 1.3: *"No conclusion shall be declared 'settled' within MEES. All findings exist in a state of provisional acceptance, subject to renewed challenge upon presentation of superior evidence."*
