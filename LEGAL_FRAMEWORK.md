# MEES - The One
## Legal Framework for Participation, Research, and Contribution

**Date:** 2026-08-31  
**Status:** CONSTITUTIONAL GUIDANCE — Binding on all participants  
**Purpose:** To establish the legal parameters within which all MEES activities, contributions, research, and working papers must operate. This framework protects the project, its stewards, and its participants from legal liability while preserving the principles of open participation, academic freedom, and intellectual honesty.

---

## Part 1: Project Disclaimers (Protecting MEES and Its Stewards)

### 1.1 General Disclaimer

MEES - The One is an **open constitutional research framework**. It is not a legal entity, not a government body, and not a regulated financial or political institution.

**MEES does not:**
- Provide legal, financial, or professional advice.
- Endorse any specific policy, ideology, or political position.
- Guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or validity of any contribution.
- Assume liability for the actions, statements, or research of its participants.

**MEES is provided "AS IS"** — without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including but not limited to the warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, or non-infringement.

### 1.2 Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law:

> *"In no event shall the Stewards, maintainers, contributors, or any affiliated parties be liable for any direct, indirect, incidental, special, exemplary, or consequential damages (including, but not limited to, procurement of substitute goods or services; loss of use, data, or profits; or business interruption) however caused and on any theory of liability, whether in contract, strict liability, or tort (including negligence or otherwise) arising in any way out of the use of this framework, the repository, or any contributions thereto, even if advised of the possibility of such damage."*

### 1.3 No Fiduciary or Advisory Relationship

Participation in MEES does not create a fiduciary, advisory, or professional relationship between any participant and the Stewards, maintainers, or other participants. All contributions are made voluntarily and without expectation of compensation, reliance, or professional liability.

### 1.4 Jurisdictional Compatibility

As established in the **Jurisdictional Compatibility Principle** (Step 08, Section 4):

> *"MEES adoption does not itself alter, extinguish, suspend, transfer, or subordinate any legal obligation unless the relevant parties and competent legal authority have separately provided lawful effect."*

This principle applies equally to all contributions, research, and working papers. Nothing in MEES overrides or supersedes national or international law.

## Part 2: Participant Obligations (What Every Contributor Must Agree To)

### 2.1 Lawful Conduct

All participants — whether contributing data, research, opinions, working papers, or challenges — must:

> *"Adhere to all applicable national, state, and local laws."*

This includes, but is not limited to:
- Defamation and libel laws.
- Intellectual property and copyright laws.
- Data protection and privacy regulations (including GDPR, where applicable).
- Anti-discrimination and hate speech laws.
- Export control and sanctions regulations.

### 2.2 Representation and Warranty

By submitting any contribution to MEES, each participant represents and warrants that:

1. They are **authorized** to make the contribution and grant the necessary licenses.
2. The contribution does not **infringe** on any third-party intellectual property, privacy, or other rights.
3. The contribution is **accurate and truthful** to the best of their knowledge, or clearly identified as speculative or opinion-based.
4. They have **disclosed any conflicts of interest** that may reasonably affect the credibility of their contribution.

### 2.3 License Grant

By contributing to MEES, participants agree that their contributions are submitted under the **MIT License** (or such other open license as may be designated by the Stewards) and may be used, reproduced, modified, and distributed by others in accordance with that license.

### 2.4 Indemnification

Each participant agrees to **indemnify, defend, and hold harmless** the Stewards, maintainers, and other participants from any claims, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising from:

- Their breach of this Legal Framework.
- Any claim that their contribution infringes on a third-party's rights.
- Any unlawful or negligent act committed by the participant in connection with their participation.

## Part 3: Research and Working Papers (Specific Guidelines)

### 3.1 Academic Freedom and Responsibility

MEES upholds the principle of **academic freedom** — the right of researchers to explore, question, and challenge received wisdom. However, academic freedom is accompanied by responsibility:

- Researchers must **engage with evidence and arguments**, not merely assert preferred conclusions.
- Researchers must **acknowledge sources** of ideas and arguments, including AI-generated content.
- Researchers must **distinguish between fact, interpretation, and opinion** clearly.

### 3.2 No Exculpatory Language in Consent

Where research involves human participants or their data, **informed consent documents may not contain exculpatory language** through which the participant is made to waive or appear to waive legal rights, or release the investigator from liability for negligence.

### 3.3 Voluntary Participation

All participation in MEES research is **voluntary**. Participants may:

- Decline to participate without penalty.
- Withdraw at any time without penalty.
- Request that their data be anonymized or deleted (subject to the integrity constraints of the Regenerative Ledger).

### 3.4 Cultural and Contextual Sensitivity

Researchers are expected to be **sensitive to cultural, historical, and local contexts**, particularly when conducting research in or about communities that have experienced colonialism, marginalization, or oppression. Research should be conducted with respect for the dignity and agency of all affected communities.

### 3.5 No Harm Principle

Research contributions must not:
- Incite violence, hatred, or discrimination.
- Disseminate false or misleading information with reckless disregard for the truth.
- Violate the privacy or confidentiality of individuals without their explicit consent.
- Exploit, harm, or endanger any person or community.

## Part 4: Code of Conduct (Behavioral Standards)

### 4.1 Pledge

All participants — contributors, maintainers, stewards, and observers — pledge to make participation in MEES a **harassment-free, respectful, and constructive experience** for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or viewpoint.

### 4.2 Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:
- Engage in **respectful, constructive, and technically relevant discussion**.
- Communicate with **respect, inclusivity, and constructive intent**.
- **Challenge ideas, not persons**.
- Acknowledge and **correct errors** when identified.

### 4.3 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are **unacceptable**:
- Personal attacks, trolling, or inflammatory remarks.
- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Dismissing contributions based on origin, identity, or institutional affiliation.
- Flooding the repository with spam or low-effort entries (frivolous filings).
- Attempting to manipulate consensus or voting processes.
- Publishing or disseminating content that violates applicable law.

### 4.4 Enforcement

Project maintainers and Stewards are responsible for clarifying standards of acceptable behavior and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

**Enforcement actions may include:**
- A warning.
- Temporary suspension of participation privileges.
- Permanent ban from participation.
- Referral to relevant legal authorities, where warranted.

**All enforcement actions are published to the public log** (with identifying information anonymized where appropriate) to ensure transparency.

## Part 5: Intellectual Property and Data Protection

### 5.1 Ownership of Contributions

Contributors retain **ownership** of their contributions. However, by contributing, they grant MEES and its participants a **perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free license** to use, reproduce, modify, and distribute their contributions under the terms of the MIT License (or such other open license as designated by the Stewards).

### 5.2 Data Protection

MEES is committed to protecting the privacy and data of all participants. Where personal data is collected:

- Data will be collected **only with explicit consent**.
- Data will be **anonymized** wherever possible.
- Data will be **stored securely** and in compliance with applicable data protection laws (including GDPR, where relevant).
- Participants may **request access, correction, or deletion** of their data at any time (subject to the integrity constraints of the Regenerative Ledger).

### 5.3 Third-Party Rights

Contributors must not submit material that infringes on the intellectual property, privacy, or other rights of third parties. If a contribution includes third-party material, the contributor must have obtained the necessary permissions and must clearly attribute the source.

## Part 6: International Legal Considerations

### 6.1 Cross-Boundary Research

MEES is an international research framework. Participants are expected to:

- Comply with the **national law** of the country where they are based or employed.
- Respect the **sovereignty and legal frameworks** of countries where they conduct research.
- Adhere to internationally recognized research integrity principles, such as those articulated in the **Montreal Statement on Research Integrity in Cross-Boundary Research Collaborations**.

### 6.2 GDPR Compliance (EU Participants)

For participants subject to the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**:

- Personal data will be processed **lawfully, fairly, and transparently**.
- Data will be collected for **specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes**.
- Data will be **minimized** and stored only as long as necessary.
- Participants have the right to **access, rectify, erase, and port** their data.
- Data transfers outside the EU will be conducted with **appropriate safeguards** (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses).

### 6.3 US Participants

For participants subject to US law:

- Contributions are protected by **First Amendment** rights to free speech and academic freedom.
- However, contributions must not violate **US defamation, copyright, or export control laws**.
- Research involving human subjects must comply with **Institutional Review Board (IRB)** requirements, where applicable.

### 6.4 Indonesian and Other Non-Western Participants

MEES respects the **legal and cultural frameworks** of all countries. Participants from non-Western jurisdictions are encouraged to:

- Ensure their contributions comply with **local laws and cultural norms**.
- Identify any **culturally sensitive** material and handle it with appropriate care.
- Exercise their **academic freedom** responsibly and respectfully.

## Part 7: Dispute Resolution

### 7.1 Internal Resolution

Disputes arising from contributions or conduct within MEES shall be resolved through the **Conflict Resolution Protocol** (Step 10, Amendment 1):

| Level | Body | Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Local Stewards (informal mediation) | Minor disputes |
| **Level 2** | Arbitration Panel (formal) | Disputes within a Microclimate |
| **Level 3** | Global Participant Vote (appeal) | Cross-Microclimate or constitutional disputes |
| **Level 4** | SDS Stewards (final review) | Constitutional integrity or existential threats |

### 7.2 External Legal Recourse

Nothing in this Legal Framework prevents any participant from seeking **external legal recourse** in accordance with applicable law. However, participants are encouraged to exhaust internal resolution mechanisms before pursuing external remedies.

## Part 8: Acceptance and Agreement

### 8.1 Implied Acceptance

By participating in MEES — whether by submitting a contribution, opening an issue, proposing a change, or engaging in any activity within the MEES ecosystem — each participant **implicitly agrees** to be bound by this Legal Framework.

### 8.2 Explicit Acceptance (Recommended)

For formal contributions (including research papers, working papers, and data submissions), participants may be required to **explicitly accept** this Legal Framework by:

- Checking a box on a contribution form.
- Signing a Contributor License Agreement (CLA).
- Submitting a statement of acceptance to the public log.
