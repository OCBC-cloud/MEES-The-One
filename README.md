# 🌍 MEES - The One

**A Constitutional Framework for Sovereign Nations**

MEES is an open constitutional framework designed to enable sovereign nations to restructure their economic systems — moving away from perpetual growth, redefining how value and ecological health are measured, and creating a mechanism for orderly sovereign debt reset without catastrophic collapse.

**First Principle:** Safeguard Earth and humankind.

**Current Status:** 90-day public commentary period (until November 29, 2026).

## Why Sovereign Nations?

- GDP measures production, not health. MEES provides an alternative accounting framework.
- Monetary and fiscal policies in one nation create planetary spillovers. MEES tracks and accounts for these.
- Sovereign debt accumulation often ends in crisis. MEES offers a renewal mechanism to reset before collapse.
