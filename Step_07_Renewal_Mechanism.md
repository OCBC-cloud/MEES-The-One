### 2. The Trigger Algorithm (RCI — Regenerative Capacity Index)

The RCI is calculated quarterly for each Microclimate:

**RCI = (0.25 × FI) + (0.35 × RI) + (0.25 × RsI) + (0.15 × RHI)**

| Component | Weight | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Flourishing Index (FI) | 25% | Step 05 — Health Architecture |
| Regeneration Index (RI) | 35% | Step 05 — Health Architecture |
| Resilience Index (RsI) | 25% | Step 05 — Health Architecture |
| Relational Health Index (RHI) | 15% | Step 02.5 — Spillover Tracking |

**Thresholds:**
- **RCI ≥ 70:** Green — Healthy operation. Normal.
- **RCI 60–69:** Yellow — Monitor. Stewards prepare contingency plans.
- **RCI < 60 for 1 quarter:** Orange — Alert. Review initiated.
- **RCI < 60 for 2 consecutive quarters:** **RED — RENEWAL TRIGGERED.**
