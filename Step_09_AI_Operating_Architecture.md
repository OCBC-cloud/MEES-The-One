### 1. The AI Orchestrator Principle

The AI Orchestrator is **not an oracle**. It is a **constitutional witness**.

It does not decide. It observes, challenges, models, and publishes — and then waits for human validation.

**Core Functions:**
- **Observe:** Aggregate and analyse data from the Regenerative Ledger
- **Challenge:** Convene multiple AI models to debate interpretations and flag blind spots
- **Model:** Generate simulations and counterfactuals
- **Publish:** Stream all transcripts to the public log in real-time
- **Trigger:** Emit alerts (but never execute actions) when thresholds approach
