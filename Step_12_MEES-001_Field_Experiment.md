### 1. The Launch Principle

MEES-001 is not a pilot.

It is the **first iteration** of a living system.

It is designed to:
- Generate real-world evidence
- Test the framework under actual conditions
- Expose weaknesses and blind spots
- Inform the design of MEES-002, MEES-003, and beyond

**Launch is not the end. It is the beginning of learning.**
