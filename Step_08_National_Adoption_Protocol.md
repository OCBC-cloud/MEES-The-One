## MEES - The One
### Step 08: National Adoption Protocol
**Status:** CONSTITUTIONAL PROPOSAL  
**Purpose:** To describe how a sovereign nation may voluntarily adopt the MEES constitutional framework. MEES does not solicit, recruit, or manage such adoptions—it exists as a public good for nations to consider.

---

### 1. The Adoption Principle

MEES is a constitutional framework for sovereign nations. A nation may choose to adopt MEES if its government, parliament, or relevant governing body finds the framework compelling. MEES has no role in adoption other than providing the canonical record.

### 2. What a Nation Adopts

By adopting MEES, a nation agrees to:
- Use the MEES Health Architecture (Flourishing, Regeneration, Resilience, Renewal) as supplementary indicators alongside GDP.
- Implement the Regenerative Ledger for national accounting (tracking natural capital, unpaid care, and ecological flows).
- Establish a national Renewal Mechanism for orderly sovereign debt reset (with appropriate legal and parliamentary safeguards).
- Publish national data transparently in accordance with Constitutional Article II.

### 3. Sub-National Pilots

A nation may choose to pilot MEES at the sub-national level (a state, province, city, or cooperative) before full national adoption. Such pilots are considered "test zones" and are documented separately, but the constitutional principles remain the same.

### 4. Jurisdictional Compatibility Principle

MEES adoption does not itself alter, extinguish, suspend, transfer, or subordinate any legal obligation unless the relevant parties and competent legal authority have separately provided lawful effect.

Adopting nations must identify which MEES mechanisms are legally operative, contractually voluntary, or purely informational within their jurisdiction.
