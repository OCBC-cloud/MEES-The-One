# Code of Conduct for MEES - The One

MEES is committed to fostering an open, respectful, and evidence-driven intellectual environment. This code of conduct applies to all contributors, reviewers, and participants.

## Our Principles

1. **Evidence is the Sole Currency:** Contributions are judged by their reasoning and evidence, not by the credentials or status of the contributor.
2. **Challenge the Idea, Not the Person:** Robust challenge is encouraged, but personal attacks, ad hominem, and harassment are not tolerated.
3. **Intellectual Honesty:** A failed argument is a successful contribution if it prevents others from repeating the same error. We celebrate correction.
4. **Respect for Pluralism:** MEES acknowledges that different cultures, legal systems, and value systems exist. Contributors are expected to engage with these differences respectfully.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks, trolling, or inflammatory remarks.
- Dismissing contributions based on origin, identity, or institutional affiliation.
- Flooding the repository with spam or low-effort entries (frivolous filings).
- Attempting to manipulate the consensus or voting processes.

## Enforcement

- Minor violations may result in a warning from the Stewards.
- Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent suspension of participation privileges, as adjudicated by the Open Participation Protocol (Step 10).
- Decisions are published to the public log for transparency.

This code is derived from the MEES Constitution (Step 01, Articles I–IV) and the Open Participation Protocol (Step 10).
