## MEES - The One
### Step 02: Existing World Scan (DRAFT)
**Status:** Initial Mapping — Open for Challenge & Addition  
**Purpose:** Prevent reinvention. Identify proven, failed, and untested economic mechanisms.

---

**CATEGORY A: Full-System Alternatives (Macro)**

| Framework | Core Mechanism | Known Failure Mode | MEES Relevance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Doughnut Economics** (Raworth) | Ceiling (planetary boundaries) + Floor (social foundations). | Lacks a clear transition mechanism from current growth models. Weak on financial capital allocation. | Directly aligns with 🌱 Flourishing. Provides excellent boundary conditions for Microclimates. |
| **Steady-State Economy** (Daly) | Stable population/capital stock; throughput limited to renewable rates. | Politically unpalatable; lacks a "renewal" mechanism for structural debt/financial dysfunction. | Aligns with 🌊 Growth (limited) and 🛡️ Resilience. Gap: Renewal mechanism missing. |
| **Degrowth** (Hickel, Kallis) | Planned reduction of energy/resource use in wealthy nations. | Often perceived as austerity; lacks a coherent investment model for regeneration. | Aligns with ♻️ Regeneration. High friction with existing financial systems. |
| **Circular Economy** (Ellen MacArthur) | Waste = Food; technical/biological cycles. | Often focuses on recycling efficiency rather than *economic* value redistribution. | Strong mechanism for ♻️ Regeneration. Weak on 🌱 Flourishing (social equity). |
| **Ecological Economics** | Embodied energy/emergy as value basis. | Extremely complex to operationalize at community scale. | Useful for **Regenerative Ledger** (Step 06) but too heavy for Microclimate 001. |

**CATEGORY B: Financial & Measurement Innovations (Meso)**

| Innovation | Core Mechanism | Known Failure | MEES Relevance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **B-Corp Certification** | Legal mandate for stakeholder (not just shareholder) value. | Relies on audit/trust; does not change *money creation*. | Useful governance shell for Microclimates. |
| **Complementary Currencies** (Bristol Pound, WIR) | Local/scoped currencies to keep value circulating locally. | Often fail to achieve scale; struggle with tax compliance. | Directly relevant to **Regenerative Ledger** (Step 06). |
| **Time Banking** | 1 hour = 1 hour, regardless of skill. | Fails for high-skill/complex tasks; tends to collapse under specialization. | Low priority for Microclimate 001, but useful for community resilience. |
| **Carbon Pricing / Cap-and-Trade** | Prices externalities. | Captured by speculation; price volatility; doesn't actually reduce absolute throughput. | Identified as **insufficient** for MEES. Needs replacement/modification. |
| **Natural Capital Accounting** | Puts dollar value on ecosystems. | Morally controversial; commodifies nature. | Use with caution. MEES should prioritize *regenerative capacity* over *monetary valuation*. |

**CATEGORY C: Real-World Experiments (Micro)**

| Experiment | Location & Years | Key Outcome | MEES Insight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Mondragon Corporation** | Spain, 1956–present | Worker-owned cooperative federation. Survived multiple crises. | Proves 🌱 Flourishing + 🛡️ Resilience are achievable without external capital extraction. |
| **Costa Rica's PES (Payments for Ecosystem Services)** | Costa Rica, 1997–present | Reversed deforestation. Economy grew while forest cover increased. | Proves ♻️ Regeneration can coexist with modest 🌊 Growth. |
| **Transition Towns Movement** | Global, 2005–present | Community-led localization; mixed success. Many fizzled out. | Shows that **renewal** (🔄) is the weakest link—communities lack mechanisms for orderly restructuring. |
| **Cleveland Model** (Evergreen Cooperatives) | USA, 2008–present | Anchor institutions + worker co-ops + local hiring. | Strong resilience, but struggled to scale beyond anchor-institution dependency. |
| **Kerala's "People's Plan"** | India, 1996–present | Decentralized participatory budgeting. | Proves open participation can work, but requires massive institutional infrastructure. |

**SYNTHESIS: WHAT IS MISSING (GAP IDENTIFICATION)**

After scanning the above, MEES must solve what no existing framework solves:

1. **The Renewal Gap:** How does a community *reset* accumulated bad debt, obsolete infrastructure, or exhausted resources *without* catastrophic collapse?
2. **The Ledger Gap:** How do we measure value that includes regeneration and resilience, not just throughput?
3. **The AI Stewardship Gap:** How do we deploy autonomous AI to *witness* and *challenge* without becoming the *decider*? (We solved this in Step 01, but the software doesn't exist yet.)
