### Step 05: Health Architecture (MEASUREMENT SPEC)
**Status:** DRAFT — Operational Indicators for Flourishing, Regeneration, Resilience & Renewal  
**Purpose:** Define the measurable indicators that will populate the Regenerative Ledger and trigger the Renewal Mechanism.

---

### 1. The Health Architecture Principle

A healthy economic organism cannot be measured by a single number.

Instead, MEES adopts a **dashboard of indicators** across four domains:

1. **Flourishing** — Human wellbeing and capability
2. **Regeneration** — Ecological health and restoration
3. **Resilience** — Capacity to absorb and adapt to disturbance
4. **Renewal** — Triggers and thresholds for orderly reset

Each indicator is:
- **Measurable** (quantitative or qualitative with clear methodology)
- **Verifiable** (open to independent audit)
- **Comparable** (consistent across Microclimates, while allowing local variation)
- **Actionable** (changes in the indicator inform economic decisions)

---

### 2. Domain 1: Flourishing (Human Wellbeing)

**Principle:** Flourishing is not GDP per capita. It is the capacity of individuals and communities to live with dignity, purpose, and agency.

| Indicator | Measure | Data Source | Frequency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Life Expectancy** | Years at birth | Local health records | Annual |
| **Self-Reported Life Satisfaction** | 0–10 scale | Survey (validated instrument) | Quarterly |
| **Social Connection** | % reporting strong community ties | Survey | Quarterly |
| **Agency & Participation** | % participating in local decision-making | Participation records | Annual |
| **Material Security** | % with adequate food, housing, healthcare | Local records / survey | Quarterly |
| **Education & Capability** | % with access to skill development | Local records | Annual |
| **Mental Health** | % reporting low psychological distress | Survey | Quarterly |
| **Unpaid Care Recognition** | Hours of unpaid care work recorded | Time-use survey | Annual |

**Composite Flourishing Index (FI):** Weighted average of the above (0–100).  
**Warning Threshold:** If FI falls below 60 for two consecutive quarters, a Flourishing Review is triggered.

---

### 3. Domain 2: Regeneration (Ecological Health)

**Principle:** Regeneration means actively restoring the ecological systems upon which the economy depends — not merely reducing harm.

| Indicator | Measure | Data Source | Frequency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Soil Organic Matter** | % organic content in local soils | Soil testing | Annual |
| **Biodiversity Index** | Species richness / baseline | Ecological survey | Annual |
| **Water Quality** | Chemical/biological purity (0–100) | Water testing | Quarterly |
| **Air Quality** | PM2.5 / NO2 levels | Monitoring stations | Monthly |
| **Forest/Cover Change** | Net change in tree canopy | Satellite / ground survey | Annual |
| **Regenerative Investment** | % of local capital flowing to restoration | Ledger tracking | Quarterly |
| **Ecological Footprint** | Hectares per capita | Consumption data | Annual |
| **Carbon Drawdown** | Net CO2 sequestered (tonnes) | Direct measurement / proxy | Annual |

**Composite Regeneration Index (RI):** Weighted average of the above (0–100).  
**Warning Threshold:** If RI falls below 65 for two consecutive quarters, Regeneration Review is triggered.

---

### 4. Domain 3: Resilience (Systemic Capacity)

**Principle:** Resilience is the capacity to absorb disturbance, adapt, and continue functioning — without catastrophic collapse.

| Indicator | Measure | Data Source | Frequency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Economic Diversity** | Herfindahl index (1 = diversified, 0 = concentrated) | Sectoral employment data | Annual |
| **Debt-to-Regeneration Ratio** | Total debt / Annual Regeneration Investment | Ledger | Quarterly |
| **Local Food Production** | % of food consumed produced locally | Supply chain data | Annual |
| **Energy Independence** | % of energy from local renewable sources | Energy records | Quarterly |
| **Social Trust** | % reporting trust in neighbours/institutions | Survey | Quarterly |
| **Infrastructure Robustness** | Condition score (0–100) of critical infrastructure | Inspection records | Annual |
| **Skill Redundancy** | % of workforce with transferable skills | Labour records | Annual |
| **Relational Health (RHI)** | Positive Spillovers / Negative Spillovers | Ledger / external records | Annual |

**Composite Resilience Index (RsI):** Weighted average of the above (0–100).  
**Warning Threshold:** If RsI falls below 60 for two consecutive quarters, Resilience Review is triggered.

---

### 5. Domain 4: Renewal (Orderly Reset Triggers)

**Principle:** Renewal is not collapse. It is a planned, orderly reset of accumulated dysfunction — before it becomes catastrophic.

**The Regenerative Capacity Index (RCI)** is the **primary trigger** for Renewal.

| RCI Component | Weight | Measure |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flourishing Index (FI)** | 25% | 0–100 |
| **Regeneration Index (RI)** | 35% | 0–100 |
| **Resilience Index (RsI)** | 25% | 0–100 |
| **Relational Health Index (RHI)** | 15% | 0–100 |

**RCI = (0.25 × FI) + (0.35 × RI) + (0.25 × RsI) + (0.15 × RHI)**

**Thresholds:**
- **RCI ≥ 70:** Healthy. Normal operation.
- **RCI 60–69:** Monitor. Review plans.
- **RCI < 60 for 2 consecutive quarters:** **RENEWAL WINDOW TRIGGERED.**

---

### 6. The Renewal Window Protocol (Operational)

When RCI < 60 for two consecutive quarters:

1. **Auto-Trigger:** The AI Orchestrator flags the trigger to the public log.
2. **Dead-Man's Switch:** 72-hour countdown begins for human stewards to confirm or override.
3. **Renewal Council Convened:** 7 random participants + 2 AI witnesses audit the ledger.
4. **Legacy Debt Frozen:** All outstanding financial/material/energetic obligations are paused.
5. **Renewal Credits Issued:** Credits are allocated to actors committing to regenerative actions.
6. **Discount Period:** 1 Renewal Credit = 1.2 units of legacy debt (incentive to participate).
7. **90-Day Window:** After 90 days, the ledger is cleansed. RCI resets to baseline 0.85.
8. **Public Report:** All actions, decisions, and outcomes are published to the canonical record.

---

### 7. Data Collection and Validation

**Provenance Requirement:** Every data point must include:
- Source (human, sensor, AI, or combination)
- Timestamp
- Methodology (if survey: instrument; if sensor: model and calibration)
- Confidence interval (if applicable)

**Verification Layer:** Any participant may challenge a data point. The challenge triggers an independent re-measurement or audit. The result is appended to the record, not overwritten.

---

### 8. The Health Dashboard (Public-Facing)

Each Microclimate shall publish a **public health dashboard** showing:

- Current FI, RI, RsI, RHI, and RCI
- Trend lines over time (quarterly, annual)
- Alert status (Green / Yellow / Red / Renewal Window)
- Link to the full public ledger
- Link to the AI debate transcripts on any flagged issues

This dashboard is the **visible face** of the Health Architecture — making the health of the economic organism accessible to all participants.
