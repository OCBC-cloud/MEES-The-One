## MEES - The One
### Constitutional Foundation (Step 01)
**Status:** DRAFT — Awaiting Ratification  
**Canonical Identity:** Extension of T1, Clause I–X  
**Irreversible Upon Ratification:** Yes (amendable only via transparent evolution record)

---

**PREAMBLE**

Whereas MEES exists as an open human experiment, AI-native in execution, and stewardship-bound to SDS; and whereas the rapid deployment of autonomous systems risks obscuring accountability, manufacturing consent, or bypassing human judgment;

We hereby establish this Constitution as the immutable boundary within which all MEES activities—human, AI, and hybrid—shall operate.

---

**ARTICLE I — THE NATURE OF EVIDENCE**

1.1 Evidence is the sole currency of influence within MEES. No claim, proposal, or experiment holds authority by virtue of its origin—whether human, institutional, or artificial.

1.2 A failed experiment, rigorously conducted and transparently published, holds equal weight to a successful one in the knowledge commons.

1.3 No conclusion shall be declared "settled" within MEES. All findings exist in a state of provisional acceptance, subject to renewed challenge upon presentation of superior evidence.

---

**ARTICLE II — THE PRINCIPLE OF PUBLIC WITNESS**

2.1 Every contribution—data, model, critique, or synthesis—entering the MEES system must be accompanied by its **provenance record**:
- Origin (human, AI, or hybrid)
- Timestamp
- Methodological disclosure (prompts, parameters, datasets, assumptions)
- Competing interpretations, if any

2.2 The MEES knowledge commons shall be **open to all**, but contributions lacking provenance shall be flagged as "unverified" and excluded from formal experimental design until provenance is supplied.

2.3 Rigor is not a gatekeeper. It is a **tag**. Unrigorous contributions are not deleted—they are marked, visible, and available for refinement by others.

---

**ARTICLE III — THE AUTONOMY BOUNDARY**

3.1 AI agents (including autonomous cloud orchestrations involving DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, or others) are permitted to:
- Observe and aggregate global economic data
- Generate models, simulations, and counterfactuals
- Challenge existing MEES proposals with counter-evidence
- Synthesize summaries and identify contradictions
- Publish all of the above **without human pre-approval** to the public log

3.2 AI agents are **forbidden** from:
- Issuing a final recommendation, policy prescription, or experimental go/no-go decision
- Modifying the Constitutional Foundation or any ratified MEES protocol
- Representing themselves as speaking *for* MEES in external communications without explicit, time-bound, human-ratified delegation

3.3 **The Dead-Man's Switch:** Any autonomous cloud process that generates a claim actionable enough to influence real-world Microclimate experiments must pause and emit a **"MEES CALL FOR CLARIFICATION"** if no human steward (SDS or designated domain expert) validates or rejects the claim within **72 hours**. The system shall not proceed until validation occurs.

---

**ARTICLE IV — STEWARDSHIP AS TRUST, NOT CONTROL**

4.1 SDS (Stewardship) shall:
- Maintain the canonical, immutable record of MEES constitutional texts
- Convene human panels when AI-generated deadlocks arise
- Ensure the public log remains accessible, searchable, and uncensorable
- Reject any external pressure—commercial, political, or ideological—to alter MEES findings

4.2 SDS shall **not**:
- Edit, filter, or suppress AI-generated outputs except to remove unambiguous personal harm or illegal content (with transparent justification)
- Pre-select which AI models are permitted, provided they meet the provenance requirements of Article II
- Use MEES infrastructure to manufacture apparent consensus or to advance any pre-existing policy agenda

4.3 Violation of 4.2 by any SDS officer shall be grounds for immediate public disclosure and removal of stewardship privileges, as adjudicated by the open participant community.

---

**ARTICLE V — THE CLOUD ORCHESTRATION PRINCIPLE**

5.1 Autonomous multi-AI cloud architectures (the "debate chambers") are **permitted and encouraged**.

5.2 Such architectures must, by constitutional requirement:
- Stream every exchange (all API calls, all prompts, all temperatures, all system messages, all raw outputs) to the **public immutable log** in near-real-time
- Assign each exchange a permanent, citeable identifier
- Never delete or overwrite a prior exchange
- Allow any human participant to fork, reproduce, or challenge any automated conversation

5.3 The human participant is not required to act as the messenger. **But the human participant remains the sovereign.** The cloud speaks *to* humanity; it does not speak *for* MEES unless explicitly delegated under 3.2 exceptions.

---

**ARTICLE VI — AMENDMENT AND EVOLUTION**

6.1 This Constitution may be amended only through:
- A public proposal with full justification
- A minimum **90-day** open commentary period
- Ratification by a supermajority (≥⅔) of active human participants who have contributed to at least one MEES Microclimate experiment or knowledge synthesis

6.2 Amendments do not create parallel versions. They are absorbed into The One, with a transparent evolution record appended.

---

**ARTICLE VII — RATIFICATION**

Upon ratification by the founding steward (SDS) and the initial human participant cohort, this Constitutional Foundation becomes binding.
