### 7. Conflict Resolution Protocol

**7.1 The Conflict Resolution Principle**

Disputes are inevitable in any human system. MEES does not aim to eliminate conflict—it aims to resolve it fairly, transparently, and without retaliation.

**All disputes shall be resolved through the following escalation pathway:**

| Level | Body | Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Local Stewards (informal mediation) | Minor disputes within a Microclimate |
| **Level 2** | Arbitration Panel (formal) | Disputes within a Microclimate that cannot be resolved informally |
| **Level 3** | Global Participant Vote (appeal) | Disputes that cross Microclimates or involve constitutional interpretation |
| **Level 4** | SDS Stewards (final review) | Disputes involving constitutional integrity or existential threats |

---

### 7.2 Level 1: Local Stewards (Informal Mediation)

**Applicable to:** Minor disputes (e.g., data accuracy disagreements, interpersonal conflicts, credit allocation questions).

**Process:**
1. Any participant may raise a concern with the local stewards.
2. The stewards shall convene a mediation session within 14 days.
3. Both parties are heard. The stewards may propose a non-binding resolution.
4. If the resolution is accepted, the dispute is closed and recorded in the public log.
5. If either party rejects the resolution, the dispute escalates to Level 2.

**Time Limit:** 30 days from initial filing to resolution or escalation.

---

### 7.3 Level 2: Arbitration Panel (Formal)

**Applicable to:** Disputes that cannot be resolved informally (data challenges, credit disputes, governance disagreements).

**Panel Formation:**
- 3 randomly selected participants (from the same Microclimate, if possible; otherwise, from any Microclimate).
- 1 AI witness (non-voting, provides analysis and transcript).
- 1 neutral observer from a neighbouring Microclimate (if available).

**Process:**
1. The dispute is submitted in writing to the panel.
2. Both parties submit evidence and arguments (written).
3. The panel convenes within 30 days.
4. The AI witness provides an analysis of the evidence (published to the public log).
5. The panel deliberates and reaches a majority decision (≥2 of 3).
6. The decision is published to the public log, with rationale.

**Standard of Evidence:**
| Claim Type | Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Data accuracy** | Preponderance of evidence (>50% likelihood) |
| **Credit allocation** | Preponderance of evidence |
| **Governance violation** | Clear and convincing evidence (>75% likelihood) |
| **Constitutional violation** | Beyond reasonable doubt (>95% likelihood) |

**Appeal:** Any party may appeal the panel's decision to Level 3 within 30 days.

**Time Limit:** 60 days from panel formation to decision.

---

### 7.4 Level 3: Global Participant Vote (Appeal)

**Applicable to:**
- Appeals of Level 2 decisions.
- Disputes that cross Microclimate boundaries (e.g., water flow disputes, spillover accounting).
- Disputes involving constitutional interpretation.

**Process:**
1. The appellant submits a written appeal to the global participant body.
2. The appeal is published to the public log.
3. A 30-day commentary period begins (open to all participants).
4. A global vote is held. All active participants (across all Microclimates) are eligible to vote.
5. The threshold for overturning a Level 2 decision is ≥⅔ of votes cast.
6. The outcome is published to the public log, with a summary of the debate.

**Time Limit:** 90 days from appeal filing to resolution.

---

### 7.5 Level 4: SDS Stewards (Final Review)

**Applicable to:**
- Disputes involving existential threats to MEES (e.g., constitutional integrity, system collapse, external legal challenges).
- Disputes that have exhausted Levels 1–3 and remain unresolved.

**Process:**
1. SDS stewards review the full record (Level 1–3 proceedings).
2. SDS stewards may consult with external legal or subject-matter experts.
3. SDS stewards issue a final, binding decision within 60 days.
4. The decision is published to the public log, with full rationale.

**SDS authority is not absolute.**
- If ≥⅔ of global participants vote to override an SDS decision, the decision is reversed.
- This ensures that SDS's final review is accountable to the participant body.

---

### 7.6 Cross-Microclimate Disputes

If two or more Microclimates dispute a cross-border flow (e.g., water, capital, spillover effects):

1. **Initial Escalation:** The dispute bypasses Level 1 and goes directly to Level 2 (Arbitration Panel).
2. **Panel Composition:** The panel shall include at least one representative from each affected Microclimate (randomly selected) plus a neutral chair from an unaffected Microclimate.
3. **Evidence:** Both Microclimates must submit their Regenerative Ledger data for the disputed period.
4. **Decision:** The panel's decision is binding unless overturned by a global participant vote (Level 3).

---

### 7.7 Protection Against Frivolous Disputes

To prevent abuse of the conflict resolution system:

- **Frivolous Filing Penalty:** If a party files a dispute that is determined to be frivolous (no reasonable basis), they may be required to contribute 10% of their accumulated Renewal Credits to a common fund.
- **Repeated Filings:** A party that files more than 3 unresolved disputes in a 12-month period may be subject to a temporary suspension of their dispute-filing rights (reviewable by SDS).

**Determination of Frivolousness:** The Arbitration Panel (Level 2) shall determine if a filing is frivolous. If so, the penalty is applied. The determination is subject to appeal (Level 3).

---

### 7.8 Conflict Resolution Log

All conflict resolution proceedings (Levels 1–4) shall be:
- **Published** to the public log (with personal identifying information anonymized).
- **Timestamped** and cryptographically anchored (per Step 09 Amendment 1).
- **Searchable** for future reference.

This creates a body of precedent that informs future disputes, reducing arbitrariness and building institutional memory.

---

### 8. Rationale for This Amendment

Kimi correctly identified that "evidence is the sole currency" is meaningless without a mint. This protocol provides:

- **A clear, non-arbitrary escalation pathway** (from informal mediation to global vote to SDS review).
- **A standard of evidence** that scales with the seriousness of the claim.
- **Cross-Microclimate dispute resolution** (critical for a multi-Microclimate system).
- **Protection against frivolous filings** (preventing abuse).
- **Full transparency** (all proceedings are published).

This transforms MEES from a system that *hopes* disputes will be resolved fairly to one that *guarantees* a fair, transparent, and accountable process.
