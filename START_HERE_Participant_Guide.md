# 🌍 START HERE: A 1-Page Guide to MEES - The One

**For Participants, Communities, and Curious Humans**

*"Let all contribute. Let evidence decide. Let Earth and humankind come first."*

---

## 1. What is MEES?

MEES is a **voluntary experiment** for communities who want to try a better way of doing economics. 

We believe an economy should:
- Help people **flourish** (not just produce more stuff).
- **Regenerate** nature (not just take from it).
- Be **resilient** (able to handle shocks without collapsing).
- Have a **renewal** mechanism (a way to reset bad debt without destroying everything).

**You don't need to be an economist.** You just need to care about your community and your land.

---

## 2. What do I actually have to do?

If you join a MEES Microclimate, you agree to:

1. **Track your flows** – Share basic data about what you produce, consume, or care for (anonymized). We call this the *Regenerative Ledger*.
2. **Take a survey** – A few times a year, share how you're feeling (health, trust, wellbeing).
3. **Join the Assembly** – Show up (in person or online) to help make decisions for your community.

**That's it.** You don't have to give up your land, your money, or your rights.

---

## 3. What happens if things go bad?

We have a **Renewal Mechanism** to prevent catastrophe.

If your community's health (ecological + social) drops below a critical threshold:
- **Debt is frozen** – No one has to pay during the crisis.
- **A council is formed** – Random members of the community (not outsiders) decide what to do.
- **You earn "Renewal Credits"** – By doing regenerative work (planting trees, fixing soil, teaching skills), you can reduce your own debt.
- **The community resets** – After 90 days, everyone gets a fresh start.

**No one is punished.** The system resets to prevent collapse.

---

## 4. What are my rights?

As a participant, you have absolute rights:

| Right | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Exit Anytime** | You can leave the experiment with no penalty. |
| **Data Control** | You decide if your data stays (anonymized) or is deleted. |
| **Challenge** | You can challenge any claim, any measurement, any decision. |
| **Vote** | You have a say in how your Microclimate is run. |
| **Non-Retaliation** | No one can punish you for speaking up or leaving. |

---

## 5. Do I need to know about AI, blockchain, or algorithms?

**No.** 

We use AI behind the scenes to help us crunch numbers and spot problems—but **AI cannot make decisions**. Only humans (you and your neighbors) make the final call.

The technology is just the *engine*. You are the *driver*.

---

## 6. How do we start a Microclimate?

1. **Gather 10+ people** in your neighborhood, cooperative, or workplace.
2. **Consent** – everyone agrees to the simple rules above.
3. **Map your reality** – Who owns the land? What water do you use? What do you grow?
4. **Start tracking** – Just start recording basic data (soil, spending, happiness).
5. **Connect** – You don't need to build the software. Just send us your data and we'll help you set up the dashboard.

---

## 7. Do I have to sign a legal contract?

No. This is a **voluntary social experiment**, not a binding legal entity. 

However, if you want to make your Microclimate more formal, we provide templates for cooperative or trust structures (but that's entirely optional).
