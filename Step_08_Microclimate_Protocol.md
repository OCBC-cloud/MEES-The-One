## MEES - The One
### Step 08: Microclimate Protocol (EXPERIMENT BLUEPRINT)
**Status:** DRAFT — Launch Blueprint for Microclimate 001  
**Purpose:** Define the selection criteria, participant requirements, onboarding process, data initialization, and first-year milestones for the first real-world MEES experiment.

---

### 1. The Microclimate Principle

A Microclimate is a **bounded, living economic ecosystem** where the MEES framework is implemented, tested, and refined.

It is not a simulation. It is a real community of people, land, and economic activity — operating under the MEES Constitution and System Model.

**Microclimate 001** is the first iteration. It is designed to be:
- **Small enough** to manage and observe closely
- **Large enough** to generate meaningful evidence
- **Diverse enough** to test the framework under real-world complexity
- **Open enough** to allow transparent public scrutiny

---

### 2. Site Selection Criteria

The founding team (SDS + initial stewards) shall identify a candidate site based on the following criteria:

| Criteria | Weight | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Population Size** | High | 50–200 participants (enough for statistical significance, small enough for governance) |
| **Geographic Boundary** | High | Clear physical boundary (neighbourhood, village, campus, farm cooperative) |
| **Economic Diversity** | Medium | Mix of livelihoods (farming, small business, services, informal) |
| **Ecological Significance** | Medium | Presence of natural assets (soil, water, forest) that can be regenerated |
| **Participant Readiness** | High | Demonstrated interest and willingness to commit to a 3-year experiment |
| **Institutional Support** | Low | Existing local institution (school, cooperative, council) willing to host |
| **Accessibility** | Low | Reasonable access for observation and verification |

**No single site will be perfect.** The goal is to select a site that is **good enough** — and then iterate.

---

### 3. Participant Recruitment and Consent

**Recruitment Criteria:**
- Open to all adults in the geographic boundary
- Participants may be individuals, households, or enterprises
- No minimum wealth, education, or skill requirement
- Participants must agree to:
  - Share basic economic data (anonymized for privacy)
  - Participate in periodic surveys (health, wellbeing, trust)
  - Allow their transactions to be recorded in the Regenerative Ledger
  - Accept the Renewal Mechanism (if triggered)

**Informed Consent Protocol:**
- All participants receive a plain-language explanation of MEES
- Consent is recorded (signed or digital) and stored in the canonical record
- Participants may withdraw at any time (data remains, but no new data collected)
- No personal data is published without explicit consent (aggregated and anonymized by default)

**Incentives:**
- Participants receive access to the Regenerative Ledger (visibility into their own economic impact)
- Participants are eligible for Renewal Credits (if triggered)
- Participants contribute to a public good — building evidence for a new economic system

### 4. Onboarding Timeline (90 Days)

**Phase 0 (Pre-Launch): Site Selection and Setup** | Days -90 to -30
- Identify candidate site
- Conduct feasibility assessment
- Secure local institutional partnership
- Obtain ethical approval (if required)

**Phase 1 (Launch): Participant Onboarding** | Days -30 to 0
- Recruit participants (information sessions)
- Record informed consent
- Conduct baseline surveys (health, trust, economic activity)
- Establish governance structures (local stewards, participant assembly)

**Phase 2 (Initialization): Ledger Setup** | Days 0 to 30
- Create initial nodes (people, soil plots, water sources, institutions)
- Record baseline health scores (soil, water, community)
- Establish data collection protocols (sensors, surveys, records)
- Commission the AI Orchestrator (read-only mode initially)

**Phase 3 (First Cycle): Observation and Measurement** | Days 30 to 365 (Year 1)
- First RCI calculation (Quarter 1)
- Quarterly health assessments
- Public dashboard goes live
- First participant assembly convened
- First AI debate loop activated

---

### 5. Governance Structure for Microclimate 001

**Local Stewards (3–5 people):**
- Elected by participants
- Responsible for day-to-day oversight
- Serve as conduit to SDS (global stewardship)
- Cannot serve as Renewal Council members

**Participant Assembly:**
- All active participants
- Meets quarterly (in person or virtual)
- Reviews health dashboard
- Raises challenges and proposals
- Elects local stewards

**Renewal Council (as defined in Step 07):**
- Activated only upon RCI trigger
- Randomly selected from participants
- Disbanded after 90-day window

**AI Witnesses:**
- Non-voting
- Publish all transcripts

---

### 6. Data Initialization (Setting Up the Ledger)

**Initial Nodes (at launch):**
- All participants → `HUMAN` nodes
- All physical plots → `SOIL` and `FOREST` nodes
- Major water bodies → `WATER` nodes
- Participant enterprises → `INSTITUTION` or `CAPITAL` nodes
- Community knowledge assets → `KNOWLEDGE` nodes

**Initial Edges (at launch):**
- Known economic flows (employment, trade, energy, food)
- Recorded from baseline surveys

**Baseline Health Scores:**
- Measured during Phase 1
- Establishes the initial baseline for future RCI calculations

### 8. Success and Failure Criteria

**Success (after 3 years):**
- RCI ≥ 70 for two consecutive years
- FI, RI, RsI, RHI all showing positive trends
- Participants report high satisfaction (≥7/10)
- Evidence is robust enough to inform Microclimate 002

**Partial Success (after 3 years):**
- RCI stable but below 70
- Some indicators improved, others declined
- Clear lessons for redesign (Microclimate 002)

**Failure (after 3 years):**
- RCI < 60 and Renewal Mechanism fully activated
- Participants withdraw or report dissatisfaction
- Data quality is insufficient

**Important:** Failure is not a negative outcome. It is a successful contribution to knowledge — provided it is published transparently (Constitutional Article I, 1.2).

### 10. Connection to the System Model

The Microclimate Protocol operationalizes **Layer 4 (Action)** of the System Model:

- **Layer 1 (Observation):** Ledger tracks all flows and health scores.
- **Layer 2 (Challenge):** AI Orchestrator analyzes and debates.
- **Layer 3 (Decision):** Local stewards, assembly, and Renewal Council decide.
- **Layer 4 (Action):** This protocol is the action plan for deployment.
