### Amendment 1 to Step 07: Renewal Credit Inflation Protocol
**Status:** RATIFIED (Provisionally adopted by Founding Steward)  
**Effective Date:** Immediately  
**Purpose:** To address Kimi's valid critique that the Renewal Credit mechanism has a hidden inflation problem (1 credit = 1.2 debt units expands the money supply) and lacks clarity on who bears the loss. This amendment establishes a fixed, non-inflationary unit, caps credit issuance, and defines loss-bearing rules.

### 1. Amendment to Step 07, Section 7 (Renewal Credits: Detailed Mechanics)

**Replace the existing Section 7 with the following:**

#### 7. Renewal Credits: Detailed Mechanics

**7.1 Credit Denomination (The "Regenerative Unit")**

Renewal Credits shall be denominated in a fixed, non-inflationary unit: the **Regenerative Unit (RU)** .

- 1 RU = 1 unit of regenerative value (e.g., 1 tonne of CO2 sequestered, or 1 hectare of soil restored to baseline health).
- RUs are **not** a currency. They are a **measure of regenerative contribution**.
- RUs do not expand the money supply. They represent a *claim on legacy debt reduction*, not new money.

**7.2 Credit Issuance Cap**

To prevent excessive issuance and dilution, the following caps apply:

| Cap | Limit | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total RUs per Renewal Window** | ≤ 20% of the Microclimate's total legacy debt (in value terms) | Prevents debt dilution beyond a manageable level |
| **RUs per Participant** | ≤ 10% of their individual legacy debt | Prevents concentration of credit power |
| **RUs per Action Type** | Varies (see table below) | Ensures high-impact actions are prioritized |

**If demand for RUs exceeds the cap, the Renewal Council shall prioritize actions based on:**
1. **Regenerative impact** (measured by the Regenerative Ledger).
2. **Urgency** (actions that prevent imminent ecological collapse).
3. **Equity** (actions that benefit the most vulnerable participants).

**7.3 Credit-to-Debt Exchange Rate (Revised)**

The exchange rate shall be **fixed and transparent**:

- **1 RU = 1 unit of legacy debt** (not 1.2).
- The 20% discount is **removed** to prevent engineered inflation.
- Instead, the **discount is applied at the debt level**:
  - Legacy debt is **marked down** by a percentage determined by the Renewal Council (based on ability to pay and systemic fairness).
  - The markdown is **publicly justified** and published to the log.

**Example:**
- Participant owes $10,000.
- Renewal Council determines a 30% markdown (based on hardship + systemic reset).
- Participant's debt is reduced to $7,000.
- They earn RUs to pay down the $7,000 at a 1:1 rate.

**This separates the credit issuance (based on regenerative action) from the debt reduction (based on fairness), preventing inflation.**

**7.4 Who Bears the Loss?**

| Case | Loss Bearer | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Debt write-down** | Creditors (lenders) | They took the risk; it is a negotiated restructuring, not a default. |
| **Credit issuance** | No one | RUs are not money; they are a claim on debt reduction, not new currency. |
| **Unspent RUs** | Expire (return to pool) | No loss—they simply don't get used. |
| **Administrative costs** | Community fund (if available) | Shared cost of renewal. |

**Creditors are not "punished."** The markdown is a **negotiated restructuring**—comparable to bankruptcy or debt forgiveness programs. The Microclimate shall engage with creditors transparently and in good faith.

**7.5 Credit Expiration**

- RUs expire **30 days** after the Renewal Window closes.
- Unused RUs are **returned to the common pool** (they do not carry over).
- This prevents hoarding and ensures RUs are used for their intended purpose: regenerative action.

**7.6 Transparency Requirements**

All credit issuance, debt markdowns, and loss allocations shall be:
- Published to the public log.
- Cryptographically anchored (per Step 09 Amendment 1).
- Subject to challenge (per Step 10 Amendment 1, Conflict Resolution).

**7.7 The "No Inflation" Guarantee**

Because:
- RUs are not a currency (they are a measure of regenerative value),
- RUs do not circulate as money (they are claims on debt reduction),
- The total RU issuance is capped at 20% of legacy debt,
- Unused RUs expire,

**There is no expansion of the money supply.** The total stock of financial assets in the Microclimate does not increase. This prevents the engineered inflation identified by Kimi.

---

### 2. Rationale for This Amendment

Kimi correctly identified that the original "1 credit = 1.2 debt units" created a hidden inflation mechanism: every Renewal Window expanded the money supply by 20%. This amendment:

- **Removes the 20% premium** (replaced by a debt markdown that is justified separately).
- **Caps issuance** to prevent dilution.
- **Defines loss bearers** (creditors bear the write-down, not the community).
- **Uses RUs as a measure, not a currency** (no money supply expansion).
- **Adds expiration** to prevent hoarding.

This makes the Renewal Mechanism economically sound, transparent, and defensible.
