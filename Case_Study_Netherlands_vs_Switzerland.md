## The Research Question

The Netherlands and Switzerland are two small European nations with comparable populations, GDPs, and global influence. Both are highly developed, wealthy, and deeply integrated into the global economy.

**Yet their histories could not be more different:**

- The Netherlands built its wealth through **colonial extraction**, **slave trade**, and **monopoly trade** (VOC, WIC).
- Switzerland built its wealth through **banking**, **neutrality**, **precision manufacturing**, and **global trade** — without a colonial empire.

**The central question is:**

> *"Can a nation achieve sustained prosperity without colonial extraction? If Switzerland's success is a counterexample, what lessons does it offer for nations seeking a 'clean' development path? And if Switzerland's wealth is also tainted — through banking, arms trade, or resource extraction — what does that reveal about the global system itself?"*

## Historical Context

| Period | 🇳🇱 Netherlands | 🇨🇭 Switzerland |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **17th Century** | **Dutch Golden Age.** VOC and WIC establish global trade networks, spice monopolies, and slave trade. Bank of Amsterdam (1609) becomes the world's first modern central bank. | **Early Confederacy.** Swiss cantons maintain neutrality; begin developing banking and watchmaking. |
| **18th–19th Century** | **Decline and transition.** Colonial revenues decline; shift to trade, finance, and industrialization. | **Neutrality and precision.** Congress of Vienna (1815) establishes permanent Swiss neutrality. Banking, watchmaking, and pharmaceuticals emerge. |
| **20th Century** | **Post-war recovery.** Reconstruction, European integration, and modernization. | **Global banking hub.** Swiss banks become the world's largest offshore wealth managers; neutrality during both World Wars. |
| **21st Century** | **EU member.** High-tech manufacturing (ASML), logistics (Rotterdam), and financial services. | **Non-EU member.** Banking, pharmaceuticals (Novartis, Roche), and manufacturing (ABB, Nestlé). |

## Key Research Questions for Contributors

| Question | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **How did Switzerland achieve comparable wealth without colonies?** | Switzerland's success is often attributed to banking, neutrality, and precision manufacturing. Is this a replicable model? |
| **Is Switzerland's wealth truly "clean"?** | Swiss banks have held assets from dictators, arms dealers, and tax evaders. Is neutrality a cover for complicity? |
| **What role did Switzerland play in global resource extraction?** | Although Switzerland had no colonies, it financed and facilitated resource extraction elsewhere. Is this a form of indirect colonialism? |
| **Can a nation transition from colonial extraction to clean prosperity?** | The Netherlands is shifting from extraction to high-tech services. Is this a viable pathway, or does the taint of history persist? |
| **What lessons does the Switzerland model offer for post-colonial nations?** | Can a country replicate Switzerland's success without centuries of institutional development? |
