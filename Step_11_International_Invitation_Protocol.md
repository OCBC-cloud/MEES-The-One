## MEES - The One
### Step 11: International Invitation Protocol (GLOBAL OUTREACH)
**Status:** DRAFT — Recurring Outreach Mechanism  
**Purpose:** Define the recurring, open invitation to the global community — economists, scientists, engineers, business leaders, communities, governments, and international organizations — to challenge, test, improve, and participate in MEES.

---

### 1. The Invitation Principle

MEES does not recruit. It **invites**.

The invitation is not: *"Do you support MEES?"*

It is: *"Challenge it. Test it. Improve it. Participate in it."*

This is the operational expression of T1, Section VIII — International Participation.

---

### 2. Invitation Categories

| Category | Target Audience | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Academic & Research** | Economists, ecologists, sociologists, data scientists | Validate methodology; contribute evidence; propose refinements |
| **Policy & Government** | Policymakers, civil servants, international organizations | Explore policy applications; provide real-world constraints |
| **Business & Finance** | Business leaders, investors, cooperatives | Test economic viability; provide capital flow data |
| **Community & Civil Society** | Community leaders, NGOs, cooperatives, neighbourhoods | Host Microclimates; provide lived-experience evidence |
| **Technical & Engineering** | Software developers, data architects, sensor engineers | Build and improve the AI Orchestrator and Ledger |
| **General Public** | All humanity | Contribute data; challenge claims; participate in governance |

---
